Points for Right to Repair

Below are a few points you can make for Right to Repair, if you're stuck:

    Repairs are often monopolized by the manufacturers and cost businesses and consumers a lot of money.

    Repairs often have delays that can damage the reputation of a business.

    Having the right to repair saves time and money.

    The right to repair something can be a form of free expression.

    Repairshops would no longer have to turn down repairs on certain machines in fear of legal action being taken.
