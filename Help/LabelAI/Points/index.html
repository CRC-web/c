Points for Labeling AI

Below are a few of many points you can make for labeling AI content, if you're stuck:

    Labeling AI-generated content would help people who are unsure of whether the content they're consuming is AI or not.

    Consumers should have the right to know the kind of content they're consuming.

    Setting labels for AI content in place would not hinder the development of AI technology, since it would only require creators, distributers, and publishers of AI content to inform the consumer of the content they provide, which would have no effect on the tech itself.

    Labels for AI content would help greatly in the fight against disinformation without infringing on others' rights (ex. deepfakes have become a form of AI disinformation).

    Reduces incidents of false accusations using AI, and saves the resources and time spent on those incidents
