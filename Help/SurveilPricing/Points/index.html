Points against Surveillance Pricing

Below are a few of many points you can make against Surveillance Pricing, if you're stuck (do NOT copy and paste these points, put them in your own words in the email):

    Surveillance Pricing could discriminate against consumers of certain races or economic backgrounds, charging them more for certain products.

    Surveillance Pricing violates the privacy of consumers.

    Consumers are being charged unfair prices based off of their personal information, they could save potentially hundreds of dollars if Surveillance Pricing is regulated.

    Surveillance Pricing targets lower-income people, creating more of a financial burden for them.

    This pricing tactic encourages companies to take increasingly invasive and unethical methods to maximize profits.
