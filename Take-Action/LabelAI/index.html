Take Action - HB1598

Summary

Want to Help Stop AI Disinformation?

Try to help us get a law passed that would require AI-generated content to be labeled in PA!

The current bill in PA's local government (HB 1598) states that: for any instance of AI-generated content, a reasonably clear and readable label (no tiny text or low volumes, enough time for the average person to process it, etc) must be displayed for it at the first time a user can see it. These labels would need to be in the same medium or form as the content (so, text labels for AI-generated texts or videos, audio labels for AI-generated audio).

While it wouldn't completely remove AI disinformation, labeling AI content would help significantly in efforts against increasingly realistic deepfakes and other forms of AI disinformation. Additionally, consumers should have the "Right to Know" what kind of content they are consuming. Please click the button below to begin writing an email to send to your representatives.
