Take Action - HB1512

Summary

Ever wondered why McDonald's ice cream machines always seemed broken in the past?

Well, the primary culprit was actually a section of an outdated law, which enabled a certain company to disallow McDonald's own employees from repairing the machines they paid for, instead of their specialized maintenance team. The Right to Repair movement wants to fix issues like that.

Right to Repair is the idea that consumers and businesses should be provided the tools and access to products that they bought and own so that they can repair or modify the product as they wish without the threat of legal action against them. A law called the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), gives power to manufacturers to take legal action against anyone who needs to bypass a "digital lock" in order to repair or modify a product they own (DMCA § 1201).

However, there is a bill within PA's local government called HB 1512, or the 'Right to Repair' bill, which requires manufacturers to provide the necessary tools and access to products so that consumers repair the products they own (Note: There are some notable exceptions to this bill, you can read about it here).
