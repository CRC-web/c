Labels for AI Content

What Would It Look Like?

For any instance of AI-generated content, a reasonably clear and readable label (no tiny text or low volumes, enough time for the average person to process it, etc) must be displayed for it at the first time a user can see it. These labels would need to be in the same medium or form as the content (so, text labels for AI-generated texts or videos, audio labels for AI-generated audio). Finally, it would be declared unlawful to create, distribute, or publish the content without these labels.

Why Would Labeling AI Content Help?

While it wouldn't completely remove AI disinformation, labeling AI content would help significantly in efforts against increasingly realistic deepfakes and other forms of AI disinformation. Additionally, consumers should have the "Right to Know" what kind of content they are consuming (or, in the context of AI, the right to know whether the content they're consuming is AI-generated or not). By supporting the passing of a law regarding this issue, we would take the first steps in fixing both issues.
